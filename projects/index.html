Projects: Sentinel, an anti-drone detection system

For my final-year project at Instituto Superior Técnico, I was project manager of Sentinel, a six-person team that built an anti-drone detection system using machine learning. We worked with the Portuguese Air Force, Thales and Mauser.

Dec 2024 – Jul 2025LisbonBSc final-year project, Instituto Superior TécnicoRole: Project Manager, team of six

Sentinel: anti-drone detection for communication jamming

The problem. Drones are increasingly used for illicit activities, such as entering restricted airspace and carrying out unauthorized surveillance. Security forces need a reliable way to detect them and raise the alarm.

What we built. A camera-based system that watches the airspace and detects drones in real time using computer vision and machine learning. It tells drones apart from other objects, such as birds, and keeps tracking a drone once it's found. This keeps a directional jammer or antenna aimed for possible neutralization. A web app shows the system's status and sends instant alerts with a photo and a timestamp.

How it works

  • Detection model: YOLO v11, first trained on about 10,000 images and then refined to separate drones from other flying objects.
  • Hardware: built with components including a Raspberry Pi 5, with servo motors that control the camera's orientation so it can follow a target.
  • Prototype: full system integration reached in May 2025; the complete prototype was assembled and fabricated through metalwork and cutting by June 2025.

My role

As project manager, I coordinated the project, managed the team and led our work with the partners. I organized the meetings and agendas documented on the project site, including a meeting at the Portuguese Air Force's facilities in June 2025. I also produced the project's presentations and explanatory video.
