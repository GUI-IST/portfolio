## 5. Projects & Achievements

### Sentinel: Anti-Drone Detection System for Communication Jamming
- **Dec 2024 – Jul 2025** · Lisbon · BSc final-year project at Instituto Superior Técnico
- **Role: Project Manager** in a team of six. Per the project site, the role covered project coordination, team management and partner engagement. Guilherme also produced the presentations and the explanatory video, and organized the meetings and agendas documented on the project site.
- **Partners:** Portuguese Air Force, Thales, Mauser (and Instituto Superior Técnico).
- **Problem:** drones are increasingly used for illicit activities, such as entering restricted airspace and unauthorized surveillance.
- **What the team built:** a camera-based system that monitors airspace and detects drones in real time using computer vision and machine learning. It distinguishes drones from other objects such as birds and keeps tracking them, so a directional jammer or antenna can stay aimed for possible neutralization. A web app shows system status and sends instant notifications with a photo and a timestamp.
- **Technical facts (from the project blog):** YOLO v11 detection model, first trained on about 10,000 images; components include a Raspberry Pi 5; servo motors control the camera's orientation; full system integration in May 2025; complete prototype assembled and fabricated (metalwork and cutting) by June 2025; a meeting at the Portuguese Air Force's facilities on 4 June 2025.
- **Proof:** project website https://sentinel-anti-drone.vercel.app/, with an explanatory video (YouTube), project proposal, midterm and final pitch decks, and 14 weekly blog posts (Feb–Jun 2025).
