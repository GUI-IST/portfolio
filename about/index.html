About

About me

I'm Guilherme Candeias Martins, a 22-year-old engineer from Cascais, Portugal, now living in Delft. Away from the lab, I've spent years volunteering in Cascais and taken part in three Erasmus+ youth exchanges. I play guitar in my own band.

What drives me

I'm motivated by challenging problems, by working with people whose skills differ from mine, and by turning theory into something that works in practice. I want to keep growing every day, as an engineer and as a person.

Volunteering

2024 – 2026

Volunteer tutor, Cascais

I gave educational support to around 15 children, from preschool to secondary school, finding different ways to explain the basics so each child could follow. Through this I discovered a passion for teaching. It played a big part in my decision to take a gap year in 2025/2026, both to explore new experiences and to spend time sharing knowledge and helping others grow academically.

2021 – 2026

Volunteer, Cascais Jovem, Cascais

I volunteered at many Cascais Jovem initiatives:

  • Occupational Safety and Health Conference: logistics and event organization
  • Children's Festival: working with children and families
  • Safe Beaches Initiative: promoting beach safety and environmental awareness
  • Portuguese elections: helping organize and coordinate the voting process
